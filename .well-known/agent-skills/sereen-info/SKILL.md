# Sereen — tDCS Depression Treatment Information

## Description
Provides AI agents with structured information about Sereen's home-based tDCS (transcranial direct current stimulation) treatment service for depression in Portugal.

## What is Sereen?
Sereen is a Portuguese health service offering home-based brain neurostimulation (tDCS) with remote clinical monitoring for treating depression. Available at https://sereen.pt.

## Treatment Details
- **Technique**: tDCS — non-invasive brain stimulation using low-intensity electrical current
- **Session duration**: 30 minutes/day
- **Protocol duration**: 10–12 weeks
- **Certification**: CE Class IIa for depression
- **Compatible with**: Current medication (complementary, not a replacement)
- **Side effects**: Mild tingling or warmth during session, no lasting effects
- **Setting**: Fully at home, guided by the app

## Clinical Evidence
- 58% remission rate after 10 weeks
- 55,000+ patients treated across Europe with home tDCS
- 90% protocol completion rate
- Backed by 200+ clinical trials and 20+ years of research
- Peer-reviewed publications available at https://sereen.pt (Ciência tab)

## How to Start
1. **Online evaluation** — 45-min video call with Sereen's psychiatrist
2. **Kit delivery** — Device + app + onboarding session (48h delivery)
3. **Daily treatment** — 30-min sessions monitored remotely by clinical team

## Target Audience
Adults in Portugal with depression who want an evidence-based, home-based complement to standard treatment.
